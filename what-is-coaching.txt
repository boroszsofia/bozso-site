What is Coaching?
For me, coaching is a creative partnership. It is a thought-provoking process centered on self-awareness: we identify your inner resources and opportunities so you can move forward with greater authenticity—whether in your career or your personal life. I don’t provide ready-made solutions, because you hold the best answers to your own business and life situations. My role is to guide you with the right questions and a structured mindset to help you find your own, most confident path.
It’s not Consulting: I don’t tell you what to do; I empower you to make your own best decisions.
It’s not Therapy: We don’t analyze the past; we focus on the present, your goals, and concrete actions.
How can I support you? (4+1 Scenarios) - Coaching is most effective when you want to level up or are facing a transition:
Leadership Challenges: New roles, promotions, or overcoming "executive loneliness."
Efficiency & Focus: Moving from operational hurdles toward strategic vision and conscious boundary setting.
Change & Stress Management: Career pivots, returning to work, or burnout prevention.
Finding Direction: Consciously building your next chapter instead of just drifting.
+1 Whatever is on your mind RIGHT NOW: If you feel stuck and need a structured, collaborative thinking process.
The Process - An action- and results-oriented journey focused entirely on you.
Duration: Typically 5–8 sessions.
Sessions: 50 minutes per session, every 1–3 weeks.
Location: Online or in person.
Session 0 (Free): A 30-minute online call to get to know each other and define the framework.
