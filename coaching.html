Coaching

A goal- and results-oriented process

Coaching is focused on deepening self-awareness. Together we explore your challenges, uncover your inner possibilities, and find your authentic answers. We focus on the present and the future — not on overanalyzing your past. I won't give you ready-made answers or solutions, but will guide you with powerful questions to help you reach your goals.

How Do We Work Together?

01 · Free Consultation

A 30-minute online conversation where we discuss what's on your mind and explore if we can work together.

02 · Goal setting

We clarify what you want to work on and what real success looks like for you.

03 · Coaching Sessions

50-minute online or in-person sessions, meeting in every 1-3 weeks.

04 · Between Sessions

Between sessions, you may receive reflective exercises and practices to deepen your progress.

Logistics

  • A coaching session is typically 50 minutes.
  • Sessions are held online or in-person in Budapest, District XIII.
  • A coaching process typically is 5–6 sessions, but I adapt to your needs.
