About me

Hi, I'm Zsófi!

ICF ACC credentialed Business & Mental Coach.

ICF ACC credentialed coach

For a long time, I believed that if I was persistent enough, worked hard, and reached bigger goals, I will feel inner peace eventually. From the outside, I was moving forward and up, but inside, the bar kept rising. After each success, a new expectation appeared.

Over time, I understood that performance is important, but true success comes from within.

This realization led me to coaching. Today, I support my high-achiever clients in seeing more clearly, connecting with their own needs, and creating sustainable ways of working, living.

Professional Background

I Understand the Corporate World!

I spent ten years in multinational environments, in leadership and regional roles. I worked in a setting where high performance, constant change, and pressure of high responsibility were part of everyday life. Most recently, I worked as Brand Director for Central Europe at Procter & Gamble.


I led multicultural, multifunctional teams, and was responsible for the strategic growth of multi-million-dollar brands. I know the pressure on decision-makers, the expectations of performance culture, and the inner tension, when someone tries to keep proving themselves for long time.

Expat Years in Switzerland

Living and Working Abroad

I spent two years as an expat in Switzerland. I know firsthand what it means to build a career in a new environment while being far from family and friends.

I moved during Covid, and despite a difficult start, it became one of the most defining and beautiful chapters of my life. It deepened my self-awareness, strengthened my mental resilience, and taught me how to build new relationships while maintaining old ones across long distances.

Sport Mental and Team Coaching

Sport and Mental Strength

Sport has always been one of the anchors in my life. I played competitive handball for thirteen years, and I still regularly challenge myself, whether through a 30-kilometer run, scuba diving, or tennis.

For me, sport is not only about endurance and physical performance, but also about the inner strength and mindset that matter both in sport and in business.

It taught me to stay focused, remain calm under pressure, and handle setbacks. These skills often appear in coaching. Building on my athletic and leadership experience, I am currently completing a Sport Mental and Team Coach qualification.

My philosophy

5 reasons (not) to work with me

01

I believe your worth is not defined by your latest achievement.

Your title, performance reviews, and results do not define your value.

02

I believe 60+ working hours per week is not a success metric.

Overtime alone does not make you more valuable.

03

I believe you can be ambitious and uncertain at the same time.

Uncertainty does not mean you are not good enough.

04

I believe real change does not come from quick hacks.

I will not tell you what to do. I will ask questions, sometimes uncomfortable ones.

05

I believe you are already enough.

In coaching, we also highlight your strengths so you can see and trust them more clearly.
