Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does it mean that you are an ICF ACC credentialed coach?

ICF - International Coaching Federation is the most recognized coaching organization in the world. ACC credentialing requires at least 60+ hours of accredited training and 100+ hours of verified client practice, both of which I exceed. It is a quality guarantee that our work follows strong ethics and a structured methodology.

2. How do I know if coaching is right for me?

If you look successful on the outside but struggle with the constant inner pressure and the need to prove yourself, this process is likely for you. Your life does not need to be falling apart; coaching helps you build a more authentic way of operating.

3. What is the difference between coaching and therapy?

A coach is like a performance trainer: we build on your current strengths to help you reach your future goals. A therapist focuses more on healing deeper wounds rooted in the past.

4. How much time does this require in a busy life?

We usually meet every 1-3 weeks for 50-minute sessions. Between sessions, you continue with practical reflections that fit into everyday life. The process is personalized, and the goal is not to add pressure but to create clarity and energy.

5. Do we meet online or in person?

We adapt to your schedule. Sessions can be fully online from anywhere in the world, and in-person sessions are also available in Budapest, District XIII.

6. What is a free 30-minute consultation?

It is a simple conversation, to discuss your current situation, clarify your questions, and I explain how I work. We check if we have the right fit for working together. It is fully free, online, and comes with no obligation.
