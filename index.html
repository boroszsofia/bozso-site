The Methodology

What is Coaching?

For me, coaching is a creative partnership. It is a thought-provoking process centered on self-awareness: we identify your inner resources and opportunities so you can move forward with greater authenticity—whether in your career or your personal life.

No instant solutions

I don’t provide ready-made solutions, because you hold the best answers to your own business and life situations. My role is to guide you with the right questions and a structured mindset to help you find your own, most confident path.

It’s not Consulting

I don’t tell you what to do; I empower you to make your own best decisions.

It’s not Therapy

We don’t analyze the past; we focus on the present, your goals, and concrete actions.
