About Me

Hello, I am Zsófi

A curious Business Coach driven by the power of growth and self-awareness; an experienced international marketing leader, and a lifelong sports enthusiast. I believe in the power of coaching because I’ve seen and personally experienced how it can shift lives and careers from a standstill, providing fresh momentum and a new sense of direction.

My Philosophy

As a coach, my promise is simple: I will support you in overcoming the challenges you’ve been struggling with alone. Yes, we will solve them – TOGETHER! Whether it’s an executive-level leadership or personal dilemma, I will guide you through a structured process within the framework of the ICF (International Coaching Federation). You bring the topic; I bring the framework.

Corporate Experience

I Understand the Corporate World

I don’t just understand, I lived it, through all its highs and lows. I bring over 10 years of international experience, most recently as a Marketing Director at Procter & Gamble. During this decade, I lived abroad, led multicultural and multifunctional teams, and was responsible for the strategic growth of multi-million dollar brands.

Been there, done that

Throughout my career, I have navigated complex market crises and celebrated major successes. I know exactly what real responsibility feels like. I have personally experienced the weight of executive expectations, the pressure, and the real risk of burnout.

Expat Experience

Living and Working Abroad

I spent two years living as an expat in Switzerland. Through this, I learned firsthand that building a career, performing in a new role, and starting a fresh life from scratch far away from your usual support system is a massive challenge.

I know what it feels like

Moving at the height of Covid meant spending my first six months in lockdown with a completely closed office. Yet, despite this tough start, it became one of the most defining and beautiful chapters of my life, teaching me self-awareness, mental resilience, and how to build new and keep old connections across thousands of kilometers.

Sport Mindset

Sport Mental Coaching

I am expanding my expertise in Sports Mental & Team Coaching to complement my business background. I believe sports are about more than physical endurance but they build the inner strength and resilience needed to manage high-pressure situations. This mindset translates perfectly from the pitch to the business world.

The Power of Mental Performance

Sports have always been my anchor for mental stamina and key energy source. Following 13 years of competitive handball, I continue to challenge myself, whether it's a 30km run, scuba diving, or tennis. Learning to maintain sharp focus, staying calm under pressure, and navigating failure all play a vital role in my coaching process.

The Methodology

What is Coaching?

For me, coaching is a creative partnership. It is a thought-provoking process centered on self-awareness. I am not giving you ready-made solutions, but we identify your inner resources and opportunities so you can move forward with greater authenticity whether in your career or your personal life.

Why work with me

True professional evolution requires more than just textbook theory. By combining an ICF-certified coaching framework with a decade of real-world leadership experience, I offer a partnership to elevate your career, impact, and personal growth.

ICF Certified Coaching

I bring the international gold standard of coaching, ensuring a highly professional and action-oriented framework.

Corporate Leadership

Managing multi-million-dollar brands and diverse teams, I know organizational complexity and executive pressure.

Expat Insights

I understand the unique emotional and professional shifts required to thrive, adapt, and build a successful life in a new country.
